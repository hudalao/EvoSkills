Meta Review of Submission7400 by Area Chair yyS3 (decision: Reject)

Summary: This paper introduces MagicMirror, a framework for artifacts assessment in text-to-image generation. Major concerns from reviewers: 1. Effectiveness of modules. 2. Alignment with human evaluations. 3. Motivation of describing artifacts and their location in natural language. 4. Imbalance of data distribution.

The rebuttal addresses a portion of the concerns. However, some major concerns are not resolved convincingly. Therefore, I suggest to reject this paper.

Reviewer Concerns: The concerns on the effectiveness of modules and the alignment with human evaluations HAVE been addressed in the rebuttal. However, the responses to the remaining important concerns such as the motivation of describing the artifacts and their location in natural language and the imbalance of data distribution are not convincing enough.

Reviewer Scores: Few reviewers participated in the discussion after the rebuttal, but did not change the score.
